Sings. Acts. Plays.

Grace Farrell is an actor-singer and soprano, based in Greater London and the West Midlands. Her stage work runs from Les Misérables for Cameron Mackintosh and the Young Vic's Orfeus: A House Music Opera to Susanna Walcott in the National Theatre's The Crucible at the Gielgud Theatre, and Eliza Doolittle in a National Theatre Studio workshop of My Fair Lady.

On screen she played Amber in the BBC's Champion, and her voice is in Blizzard's Diablo IV. In 2025 she led the international premiere of Falling in Love with Mr Dellamort at the Edinburgh Fringe. In 2026 she played Klytaimestra in Flowers for Kassandra, and joined the UK tour of Pride and Prejudice* (*sort of) as a cover, making her debut as Mary Bennet.

She sings classical, opera, folk, pop and cabaret, plays violin, guitar and piano, speaks English, French and Italian, and teaches singing.
