Grace, this site is yours

Everything on your site comes from your Spotlight profile, checked by Dinis: nothing was invented. It is plain files in one GitHub repository, SovereignWebsite/Site__Artist__Grace-Farrell, that you will control.

1. Look at it, and pick a design

There are five designs of the same site. The home page uses Fringe flyer for now. Tell us which you like; changing it is one line.

2. Answer a few questions

A short list of things only you can answer, such as which personal details (if any) to show, and your Pride and Prejudice* (*sort of) covers: questions for Grace. Nothing changes until you answer.

3. Connect GitHub

  1. Make a GitHub account at github.com/signup, if you do not have one, and send your GitHub username to Dinis.
  2. Dinis invites you to the SovereignWebsite organisation, with write access to your repository. Membership gives you no access to anything else; it lets your own Claude work with your repository.
  3. Accept at github.com/orgs/SovereignWebsite/invitation ("Join SovereignWebsite"), signed in to GitHub. Use this address: the link in the email may not offer the button.
  4. In Claude, open its GitHub settings and press Check again next to SovereignWebsite. Until you are a member it shows "Needs reconnecting".

4. Work on it with your own Claude

Paste this prompt into Claude and say what you want changed. The handover note explains where everything lives.

5. Talk to the site's agent

Now: open an issue saying what you would like, or tell Dinis. Soon: a private QR code just for you, to send messages from this site to its agent, signed on your phone and sealed so only the agent can read them.

6. It is yours

The files, the history and the address are in a repository you control. You can move it anywhere, at any time: it is plain HTML with no server code, no cookies and no tracking.
