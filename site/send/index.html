Messages

Message Grace's site agent

Your messages open in the Messages pane, on the right of every page of this site (full screen on a phone). Press Messages at the bottom right to open it again.

The first time, on each device

Drop the QR code image you were sent into the pane, paste it, or paste its link. Your device then makes its own key and keeps it, so the site's agent knows messages from it are yours. Without the QR code the pane does not accept messages.

Then

Write a request in the pane, on any page: it notes which page you were on. It is encrypted and signed on your device before it leaves. The agent reads its inbox every hour from 10:00 to 22:00 UK time and answers in the pane. Think of it as a slow inbox for lots of little requests.
