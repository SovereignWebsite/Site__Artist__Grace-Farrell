How this site was made

Everything that made this site is public, so you can check it.

The request

The site began as one request in the Sovereign Website factory: SovereignWebsite__Factory/requests. The pipeline made the repository, the address and its certificate.

The agents

The agents that work on this site are listed in data/agents.json, each with its name and its role. Each signs its commits with a key published at agents.sovereignweb.site.

Every change

  • The commits: who changed what, and when.
  • The workflow runs: each publish of the site.

Design rounds

Each design round and its review is in the design agent's back office.

Synthetic user runs

Runs of synthetic users against this site, and what they found, are listed here as the site's agents record them.
